About

I've run creative departments at agencies and in-house, and I still like making the work myself: the concept and the copy, the photo, the edit and the final file.

Husband, father of twin girls, and happiest when a project is a little bit of an adrenaline ride. I work in English and Spanish.

Eighteen years of brand systems, campaigns and content: first at McCann and Havas in Honduras, now in-house for consumer brands in Florida.

Tools

Adobe Creative Suite, Photography & video, Motion graphics, Brand systems, Social & Amazon creative.
